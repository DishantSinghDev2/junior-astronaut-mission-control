# Credits and licenses

**Lunar Outpost** is a student project by Team Pheonix for the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026 ("Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer"). It is **not made, sponsored or endorsed by NASA**. NASA names, mission names and logos that appear in NASA's own models are used only to show real hardware.

## NASA data and facts
- **DONKI** (Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information) coronal mass ejection records from NASA / CCMC, via the NASA Open APIs (api.nasa.gov, ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI). Live when reachable, otherwise an archived copy made by `tools/fetch_donki_archive.py`.
- Facts shown in events come from public NASA material: Phoenix Mars Lander (perchlorate, ice), LCROSS (Moon ice), Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter radar (Utopia ice), MOXIE on Perseverance, ISS water recovery, Veggie plant experiment, 3D Printing in Zero-G, ARISS, Human Research Program exercise, Apollo dust reports, Mars solar conjunction.
- Real landing sites: Jezero, Gale, Acidalia Planitia, Utopia Planitia, Shackleton rim, Tranquility Base.

## 3D models
- **NASA 3D Resources** (public domain, github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources): Apollo Lunar Module, Viking Lander, Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover, Habitat Demonstration Unit, Deep Space Network 34-meter antenna, Ingenuity Mars Helicopter. Converted offline for the web (Draco and WebP removed, textures resized, some meshes simplified).
- **Space Kit** by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0: base modules, corridors, props, rocks, craters.
- **Astronaut** and **Solar Panel** by Quaternius (quaternius.com, via poly.pizza), CC0 1.0.

## Textures and maps
- **Poly Haven** (polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0: ground textures (Gray Rocks, Red Laterite Soil Stones).
- **Solar System Scope** (solarsystemscope.com/textures), CC BY 4.0, based on NASA data: Mars, Moon and Earth maps. Cropped and resized for the web.
